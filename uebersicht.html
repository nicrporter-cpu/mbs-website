What's still placeholder — on purpose

Anything shown with a dashed gold underline ([X], [date], member quotes, the fee, board roles) is a board fill-in the copy deck says must not be invented. The navy image boxes and dashed Partner logo slots are intentional placeholders too — no photos or partner logos exist yet.

Legal pages (Imprint, Privacy policy, Statutes) and the social handles are marked in preparation rather than linked to nowhere. This overview is a review aid and isn't linked from the site itself.
